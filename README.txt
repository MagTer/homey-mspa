Control and monitor your M-Spa hot tub directly from Homey.

See real-time water temperature, set the target temperature, and toggle the heater, filter, and bubbles. On supported models you also get Jets, Ozone sanitizer, and UVC controls — the app matches your model against a profile so you get the controls your spa actually has.

An interactive dashboard widget, styled after the physical control panel, puts temperature, heater, filter, bubbles, jets, ozone and UVC one tap away.

Build automations with flow triggers (temperature changes, fault detected, device online and offline), actions for every control, and conditions for heater, filter, bubbles, jets, ozone, UVC and water temperature.

Supported series include Comfort, Delight, Premium, Urban, Frame, Verto, Elite, and Muse.

Available in English, German, Norwegian, and Swedish.

Setup: configure your M-Spa account under App Settings (email, password, region), then add the device from Devices > Add Device.
