Control and monitor your M-Spa hot tub directly from Homey.

See real-time water temperature, set the target temperature, and toggle the heater, filter, and bubbles. On supported models you also get Jets, Ozone sanitizer, and UVC controls — the app automatically detects your model's capabilities and only shows the relevant tiles.

Build automations with flow triggers (temperature changes, fault detected) and actions to fit your spa into your routines.

Supported series include Delight, Premium, Elite, Muse, Frame, and Urban.

Setup: configure your M-Spa account under App Settings (email, password, region), then add the device from Devices > Add Device.
